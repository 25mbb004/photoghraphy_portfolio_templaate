01The record

Nine Viennese apartments lit only by their own lamps.

An editorial commission with one rule: no strobes, no daylight, no correction. Nine apartments across Vienna photographed at night by whatever their owners actually switch on: reading lamps, stove lights, a piano lamp from 1926. Interiors magazines flood rooms with white light until every home looks like a showroom. This is the opposite: homes at their own temperature.
