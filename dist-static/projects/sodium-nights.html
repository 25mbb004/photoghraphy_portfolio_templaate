01The record

The last sodium-vapour streets of Osaka, photographed before the LED changeover.

Osaka is swapping its sodium-vapour lamps for LEDs, and an entire colour is being deleted from the city. For five months I walked the wards scheduled for conversion, photographing the orange that will not survive the decade: the night workers, the vending machines, the cats, the last orange rain. This record is an obituary for 2200 kelvin.
