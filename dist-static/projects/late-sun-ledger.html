01The record

One hillside, photographed at the same late hour for forty consecutive days.

A single hillside above Loch Slapin, photographed every day for forty days at the exact minute the sun crossed 4400 kelvin on its way down. Nothing in the frame ever moved except the light and the weather. Laid side by side, the forty frames read like a ledger of what a day is worth: an accounting system where the currency is colour temperature.
