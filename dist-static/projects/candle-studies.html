01The record

Portraits made by the light of a single flame.

Twelve sittings in the old medina, each lit by one candle and nothing else. The flame was never moved; the sitter was. What emerged was a study in how little light a face needs before it stops being a face and becomes a memory of one. Shot over nine evenings during the winter power cuts, when the city itself returned to 1859 kelvin.
