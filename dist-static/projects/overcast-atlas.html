01The record

A fashion campaign that refused to wait for the sun.

Copenhagen gives you two hundred overcast days a year; Maison Halide's autumn campaign chose to use all of them. No strobes, no silks, no sun: just the enormous grey softbox of the North Sea sky and a collection designed in silver and bone. The overcast day, usually the enemy of the fashion schedule, became the entire art direction.
